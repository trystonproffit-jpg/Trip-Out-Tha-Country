March–October 2027 · Greenville, South Carolina · 4–8 day trips · likely group size: 2–3 travelers

Research date: October 21, 2026 Goal: lowest realistic total cost Airports: GSP · CLT · ATL

Executive conclusion

Medellín, Colombia is the strongest current candidate. A 4-day trip from Atlanta with airfare around $400–$425 can plausibly land near $800–$900 per person after lodging, food, transportation, activities, and airport positioning.

Guatemala is the next-best value option. Cancún can have cheap airfare, but lodging and activities usually erase the advantage. Europe is currently not competitive unless airfare drops sharply.
