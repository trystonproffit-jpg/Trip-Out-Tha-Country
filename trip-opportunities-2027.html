Executive conclusion

Airport conclusion

  • ATL is the airport to watch for Medellín and Guatemala.
  • GSP remains worth monitoring because a $75–$100 premium can be reasonable after avoiding drive, fuel, parking, and time.

  • Frontier advertised fares must be separated from effective fares after likely carry-on or checked-bag extras.

Risks and limitations

  • Fares can disappear before booking and may be Basic Economy.
  • Carry-on or checked-bag fees could add approximately $60–$150 round trip.
  • Aggregator prices may disappear or differ at airline checkout. A fare is not verified until final checkout confirms the itinerary, fare class, taxes, baggage allowance, and total price for the required number of travelers.
  • A single cheap seat does not guarantee 2–3 seats at the same price.
  • Hotel prices vary by neighborhood, dates, and group size.
  • ATL/CLT positioning costs and budget-airline baggage fees can materially change the effective fare.
  • Totals are planning estimates, not guaranteed quotes.
  • Recheck official airline, hotel, and government entry pages immediately before booking.
